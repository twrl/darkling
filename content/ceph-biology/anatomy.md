# Ceph Anatomy

The Ceph body plan resists a single centre. Each limb is effectively a
semi-autonomous organ, and the whole organism is better understood as a
cooperating assembly than as a single animal.

## Limbs

A mature Ceph carries between six and nine limbs. Each limb regenerates over
several moults; loss is common and rarely disabling. The regenerative cycle is
touched on under reproduction in the overview.

## Neural Ganglia

Each limb houses a neural ganglion that coordinates its own movement. The
ganglia communicate through the body wall but no single ganglion commands the
others.
