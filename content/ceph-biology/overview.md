# Ceph Biology

The Ceph are a diverse group of aquatic organisms whose biology has shaped much
of the Archive's recorded history. They are distinguished by their distributed
neural architecture, regenerative limbs, and a reproductive cycle tied to the
deep currents.

## Anatomy

Ceph anatomy is distributed rather than centralised. Their limbs contain
independent neural ganglia, allowing fine motor control without reference to a
central brain. See [Ceph Anatomy](../ceph-biology/anatomy.md) for detail.

## Reproduction

Unlike their limbs, Ceph reproduction follows a single coordinated cycle,
synchronised across populations by chemical signals carried in the deep
currents. This centralised reproduction contrasts sharply with their
decentralised anatomy.
