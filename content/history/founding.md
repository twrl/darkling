# Founding of the Archive

The Archive was founded in the wake of the Tidal Wars, when the surviving
naturalists gathered what remained of the coastal records into a single
depository. Its earliest holdings are almost entirely concerned with Ceph
biology, the study that dominated the founding generation.

## The First Curators

The first curators were themselves former field naturalists. Their annotations
on the Ceph material set the house style of the Archive: terse, comparative, and
wary of certainty.
